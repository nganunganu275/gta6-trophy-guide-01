    Roadmap to the platinum

    Three stages, in order, after PowerPyx’s roadmap (difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies). The stages below are built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Your checklist progress is shown on every stage.

    Missable trophies

    Good news first: PowerPyx lists only one potentially missable trophy in the whole list. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam — but there is no mission replay, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.

    Things that look missable but are not

    • Optional missions that disappear after a while (black icons with pink letters) — they do not count for trophies.
    • Quest givers who go quiet mid-story (Kenton, for example) — every trophy-related character comes back, and all are available after the story.
    • Time-sensitive quests with an hourglass icon — they fail after a while but are not relevant for trophies.
    • Some fences disappear permanently — new ones unlock as you go, and the ones that leave have no quests.
    • A few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that later close — they also spawn elsewhere, and you do not need all of them for trophies.

    Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, “Note regarding missable content”.

    About this guide

    Vice Ledger is a free, open-source, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. It runs entirely from its own files: no account, no network, no ads. Your progress lives in your browser’s local storage and can be exported to a file and imported back.

    The list

    55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned (1 gold, 27 bronze). Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes them. Hidden flags follow PSNProfiles as broken down by EGamers.io; PSN’s tracked counts (such as 42 Shooting Range challenges, 52 areas of Leonida, 500 profile actions) come from PSNProfiles and the One More Game list.

    How the guides are written

    Every guide is written in our own words for this entry. Guides may draw on other guides, and each one names its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. Where a location or step is not pinned down by a source, the guide says so plainly (“not yet checked in the game”) instead of inventing specifics. One trophy, Bad Luck, I Guess, is still unsolved at every source we read: the guide says so rather than guessing.
